Hey there! I'm a software engineer based in [Château-Thierry][chateau],
France.

I taught myself functional programming circa 2015. My first inspiration was
the Lisp family of languages, Common Lisp in particular. Having explored
Lisp, I switched to Haskell, which looked altogether more different and
mysterious. In those early days I worked intensely and produced a lot of
open source software. My enthusiasm soon paid off: in 2016 I started my
career as a software engineer, and my very first paid job was to write
Haskell—a good deal for sure! As I gained knowledge and experience, I
authored a number of [open source projects][oss], [blog posts][posts], and
[tutorials][tutorials].

In early 2018 I joined Tweag and soon moved to France. My years there were
formative: I spent eight years at the company before resigning in July 2026.
At Tweag I met many exceptional professionals, crème de la crème in their
fields, and I hope that exposure rubbed off on me and helped me grow in my
craft. The company made me a much more well-rounded engineer. Besides
Haskell, I wrote a little OCaml professionally, then dove into Nix and Bazel.
My last four years were predominantly dedicated to Bazel work: I oversaw
large migrations and non-trivial integrations for a variety of clients,
including international giants. From February to July 2026 I led Tweag's
Scalable Builds department.
